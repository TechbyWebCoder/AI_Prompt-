01 / Introduction
Motion gives
meaning to design.
Transitions aren't decoration — they communicate spatial relationships, direct attention, and tell the story of where you've been and where you're going.
01
02 / Slide
Pages slide
into place.
The classic push transition. Content enters from the opposite direction the user navigates — intuitive, clear, and spatial. Works best for sequential content with a clear order.
02
03 / Curtain
The curtain
opens & closes.
Two panels wipe in from the center, reveal a new scene, then retract. Theatrical and deliberate — great for portfolio pieces, onboarding, and narrative flows.
03
04 / Crossfade
Dissolve between
states softly.
The gentlest transition. Content fades out while new content fades in simultaneously. Timeless, safe, and appropriate for most contexts.
04
05 / Morph
Scale, clip,
and transform.
The departing page shrinks and fades while the incoming page blooms from the center. Creates a sense of depth and hierarchy.
